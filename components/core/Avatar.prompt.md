Circular avatar for the app header, comment rows, and people lists.

```jsx
<Avatar name="مسعود رضایی" />
<Avatar name="علی رضایی" src="/photo.jpg" size="lg" />
<Avatar name="سارا" size="sm" tone="neutral" />
```

`md` (36px) is the header size. Without `src` it renders the first letter of `name` on a violet-100 disc — never a generic person glyph.
